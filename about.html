Solar and electrical supplies, with the details in view.

Jasper Solar World serves customers looking for solar equipment and electrical supplies for homes, shops, and business premises in Nigeria. Our catalogue brings product specifications, prices, and contact details together so buyers can ask informed questions before ordering.

What we sell

The catalogue includes solar panels, hybrid inverters, batteries, charge controllers, solar lighting, cables, and electrical accessories. Product descriptions identify the specifications we have for each listed item and call out compatibility details that should be confirmed before purchase.

How we approach a solar purchase

A working solar system is designed around the appliances it must support, how long those appliances run, and the conditions at the installation site. A single product rating is not enough to size a complete system. We encourage customers to discuss their loads and planned configuration with a qualified solar installer before choosing components.

The product catalogue is not an installation design. Confirm current stock, manufacturer specifications, system compatibility, delivery charges, and the final payable amount with Jasper Solar World before making a bank transfer.
