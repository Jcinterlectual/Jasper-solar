Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how Jasper Solar World handles information when you browse the store, create an account, place an order, or contact the business. Before publishing, the business owner should review this draft against its actual staff access, hosting provider, retention practices, and applicable Nigerian privacy requirements.

Information you provide

When you register, the store collects your name, email address, telephone number, and password. When you place an order, it also records the delivery address, selected products, quantities, order total, order status, and timestamps. Passwords are stored as salted password hashes and are not available in the admin customer list. Do not send payment-card details through this site.

How information is used

Account and order information is used to sign you in, prepare and fulfil orders, confirm bank-transfer payment, respond to requests, and maintain basic business records. Order information is visible to authorised store administrators for fulfilment and customer support. Jasper Solar World should restrict access to people who need it and retain records only as long as required for those purposes and applicable law.

Cookies and session storage

The store uses a first-party, HTTP-only session cookie to keep you signed in. The current application does not set advertising cookies and does not include an active Google AdSense tag. Sign out or close your browser to end your session; sessions also expire automatically.

Advertising and third-party services

The storefront currently requests typefaces from Google Fonts; its product and slider images are hosted on this website and supplied by Jasper Solar World. When your browser loads Google Fonts, Google may receive standard connection information such as your IP address and browser details. Product photos and promotional graphics are illustrative unless an image caption identifies a specific installation; they do not guarantee the exact item supplied. If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled in the future, that provider and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, or personalise ads. This policy must be reviewed and updated before enabling advertising with the actual providers, data uses, opt-out choices, and consent needed in the countries where visitors live. Learn about Google's advertising practices at Google's advertising policy and manage personalisation at My Ad Center.

Storage, security, and sharing

Account and order records are held by the store's application service so the business can operate the shop. Hosting and technical service providers may process information to provide that service. No website can promise absolute security; the operator should use HTTPS, protect administrator credentials, limit server access, maintain backups, and set a retention schedule before launch.

Your choices and requests

You may contact Jasper Solar World to request access to or correction of account details, ask about an order record, or request account deletion where legally and operationally possible. Some transaction records may need to be kept for accounting or legal reasons. Contact support@jaspersolarworld.com.ng.

Children and policy updates

This shop is for general retail customers and is not designed to collect information from children. The policy may be updated when the service or its providers change. The current version and update date will be published on this page.
