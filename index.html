Plan beyond the panel

A solar setup is a complete system.

Panels, charge controllers, batteries, inverters, cables, and protection devices have to work together. A product's headline wattage or kVA rating on its own cannot tell you how long your appliances will run.

Before comparing equipment, write down the appliances you expect to use, their rated watts, and the hours you use them each day. Share that list with a qualified installer and confirm the manufacturer's voltage and battery-compatibility requirements.

How do I choose an inverter for my appliances?

Add the running wattage of appliances you may use at the same time, then account for the starting surge of motors and compressors. Confirm continuous and surge ratings, battery voltage, and system compatibility with an installer before choosing an inverter.

How do I estimate the battery capacity I need?

List the energy you need during an outage or after sunset. Usable battery energy varies by battery chemistry, depth-of-discharge limits, temperature, age, and conversion losses. Amp-hour values also depend on system voltage, so compare watt-hours as well as the manufacturer's operating limits.

Does the listed product price include delivery or installation?

The catalogue price is for the listed product only. Delivery charges and installation are not calculated at checkout. Contact Jasper Solar World to confirm stock, delivery to your location, any installation quote, and the final amount before transferring.

How does bank-transfer checkout work?

Sign in, place the order, then use the company account details and order reference shown at checkout. Orders remain marked awaiting transfer until Jasper Solar World verifies payment. Do not rely on a transfer receipt alone as confirmation.

How can I confirm a product is compatible with my system?

Compare the product manual's DC voltage, current, battery chemistry, and power limits with the other equipment in your setup. Send the model numbers and appliance list to the shop and have a qualified installer review the proposed configuration.
