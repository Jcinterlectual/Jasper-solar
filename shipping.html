Orders, bank transfer, and delivery

Prices on the storefront are in Nigerian naira (₦) and cover the listed products. Delivery charges are not calculated by the website. Jasper Solar World must confirm product availability, delivery cost, delivery timing, and the final amount for your address before you transfer funds.

Placing an order

  1. Create a customer account and add the products you want to your cart.
  2. Enter a complete delivery address and submit the order. The order is recorded as awaiting transfer.
  3. Use the bank name and account number shown in checkout only after confirming the final amount and that the account belongs to Jasper Solar World.
  4. Include the order reference in your transfer narration. The team updates the order after checking payment.

Important: The website cannot verify bank transfers automatically. A submitted order is not proof of payment, and an order should not be marked paid until the business has checked its bank statement. Never send funds to account details shared outside the official checkout or confirmed business contact channels.

Delivery

Delivery availability, charges, and estimated dates depend on the product, stock, and destination. Contact the shop to confirm these details before payment. The order tracker displays the status recorded by Jasper Solar World; it is not a live courier map.

Changes, cancellations, and product issues

Contact the shop promptly if you need to change or cancel an order, or if an item arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered. Include your order reference and clear photos where relevant. The team will review the request and explain the available next steps under the product warranty and applicable consumer law. Confirm any product-specific warranty and return conditions before paying.
